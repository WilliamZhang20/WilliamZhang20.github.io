I am experimenting with [Mini-JEPA](https://github.com/WilliamZhang20/mini-JEPA), a small action-conditioned JEPA setup for robot control tasks in Gymnasium Robotics.

The project trains compact self-supervised world models from low-dimensional robot observations, then uses learned action priors and MPC-style planning to solve Fetch reaching, pushing, and pick-and-place tasks.
