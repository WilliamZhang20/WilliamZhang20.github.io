I have designed and taped out a custom [AI chip](https://github.com/WilliamZhang20/ECE298A-TPU) on Tiny Tapeout using Verilog and Python.

The project includes digital hardware design and a custom PyTorch compiler path, connecting model-level tooling to hardware-level execution. I have also given a [talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kccs9xk09rw) about it, thanks to GPU MODE.
